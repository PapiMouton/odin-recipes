Pesto

Ingredients

  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Garlic cloves
  • Pine nuts
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the fresh basil leaves.
  2. Peel the garlic cloves and chop them roughly.
  3. Add basil, garlic, and pine nuts to a food processor.
  4. Pulse until coarsely chopped, then add grated parmesan cheese.
  5. Slowly drizzle in olive oil while pulsing until you reach desired consistency.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

Tips

You can use a mortar and pestle for a traditional pesto preparation.
Store pesto in an airtight container, covered with olive oil to prevent oxidation.
Use pesto fresh pasta, pizza, or as a spread on sandwiches and bruschetta.
You can freeze pesto in ice cube trays for later use.

You've prepared a delicious pesto! Enjoy your meal!
