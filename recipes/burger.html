Burgers

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Chicken or beef patty
  • Lettuce
  • Tomatoes
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the chicken or beef patty on a grill or skillet for 4-5 minutes on each side.
  2. Toast the burger buns lightly.
  3. Assemble the burger: bottom bun, lettuce, tomato, patty, cheese, and top bun.
  4. Serve immediately with your favorite condiments.

Tips

You can add bacon, pickles, or onions to your burger.
For chicken burgers, season the patty with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
For beef burgers, use ground beef and form it into a patty gently.
Add mayo, ketchup, mustard, or other sauces as desired.

You've prepared a delicious burger! Enjoy your meal!
